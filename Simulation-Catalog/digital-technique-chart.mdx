The simulator shows the exposure index (EI), target EI, and deviation index (DI) on the digital image. The EI indicates detector exposure. The DI shows how far the EI is above or below the target.

We calibrated the simulator using the kVp, mAs, and source-to-image distance (SID) values in this chart. Even with these settings, the EI can differ from the target. Plate placement, object-to-image distance (OID), and collimation can all affect the EI.

Find your projection in the [upper extremity](#upper-extremity), [lower extremity](#lower-extremity), or [axial skeleton](#axial-skeleton) table.

For the confirmed projections in each module, see the [Simulation Catalog](/Simulation-Catalog/browse-by-region).
