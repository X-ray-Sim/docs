## Desktop X-ray Simulator

- [Use it in your course](/Guides/Teaching-with-Desktop/plan-your-course-rollout): choose where the simulator fits into your teaching and plan your first group.

- [Teaching activities](/Guides/Teaching-with-Desktop/activities-and-templates): try demonstrations, homework, paired practice, and image comparisons, or create images for your slides.
